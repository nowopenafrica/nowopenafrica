## 2. Security findings (live, verified)

**F1. Critical. Anyone can mint credits or a paid plan without paying.** The `payment_intents` INSERT policy is `WITH CHECK (true)` for `anon` and `authenticated`. A visitor can insert a row with `status = 'paid'`, `kind = 'credits'`, `item_id = 'pack.business'` and any `user_id`, then call the public `verify-payment` function with that reference. Its "already paid" branch runs `credit_fulfil_purchase` and `activatePlanFromIntent`. Proven in a rolled-back transaction: the anonymous insert succeeded and fulfilment returned **6,000 credits**. Nothing was written.

**F2. High. Amounts come from the client.** `PaymentModal` and `initialize-transaction` write `amount_local` from the browser, and `verify-payment` compares Paystack's charge with that same figure. A ₦50 charge recorded as ₦50 therefore passes for a ₦150,000 pack. `credit_fulfil_purchase` grants what the pack says and never compares it with what was paid. The same gap applies to plans: `planActivation.ts` never checks a price.

**F3. High. The webhook never checks the amount.** `paystack-webhook` marks an intent paid on any signed `charge.success`, with no amount or currency check. It is dormant only because it is not registered. Registering it as it stands would reopen F2.

**F4. Medium. Test payments mint real credits.** Production runs Paystack test keys. Any test-card payment would grant spendable credits, and nothing records whether a charge was test or live (Paystack's `data.domain`).

**F5. Medium (HANDOVER decision 4a).** `credit_grant_on_signup` and `credit_grant_on_plan_change` call `set_config('request.jwt.claims', '{"role":"service_role"}', true)`. That lasts for the rest of the caller's transaction, so everything after a plan change in the same transaction runs as service_role.

## 4. Plan (build on the existing ledger)

- **Phase 0, security.** Lock the `payment_intents` insert to `lead` / `initiated` with your own user id. No client-created `credits` intents. Remove the service_role claim leak from the credit triggers.
- **Phase 1, ledger v2.**
  - Credit classes, plus double-entry lines against system accounts. The cached balance is proved equal to the lines at commit, and class balances can never go negative.
  - Markets, rates and snapshots on every entry.
  - Programme flags, with closed-loop rules enforced by CHECK constraints.
  - Compliance approvals and a launch gate.
  - Backfill lines for the 18 existing entries.
- **Phase 2, money in.**
  - Server-created purchase orders with price and rate snapshots.
  - Fulfilment that requires the verified amount, currency and Paystack domain, exactly once.
  - Edge functions updated (webhook amount check for every kind).
  - Unified checkout component and wallet page.
- **Phase 3, rewards and ops.**
  - Reward rules (disabled, unpriced) with caps, budget, account age, email and idempotency; wired to business claim.
  - Refund and reversal functions.
  - Two-step adjustment workflow.
  - Config audit and admin report.
  - Settlement tables gated off.
  - Admin screen at `/admin/credits`.
