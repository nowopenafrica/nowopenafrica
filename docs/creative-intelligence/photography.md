# Photography and imagery direction

> Generated from `src/lib/create/intel/photography.ts` by `npm run intel:docs`. Edit the registry, not this file; a test fails when they differ.

The CI-0 baseline found that 28 of 35 scored designs had no photograph, and 6 of the 7 that did showed the wrong subject. Relevance comes first: a picture of the wrong thing is worse than no picture.

## Rules for every picture

- Subject first: the picture shows what the business sells, makes or is. If no such picture exists, choose an archetype that needs none.
- Never invent: no picture may show a product, place, person or result the business cannot deliver.
- One light direction and one grade across a set.
- People are shown with consent and with dignity; skin tones are exposed and graded truthfully.
- Every asset has a recorded licence (C1). AI-made images are labelled as AI and never depict real people or brands.
- Source pixels must cover the placed size at export scale: no upscaling (quality check, image quality).
- The focal point is set so a crop for another format keeps the subject.


## No picture

Type, colour and shape carry the piece.

**Do**
- Choose it on purpose, with an archetype built for it
- The headline must be specific enough to stand alone

**Never**
- An empty frame or placeholder shape where a picture was meant to be

## Full-bleed photograph

One photograph to the edges; words in its quiet area.

**Do**
- Needs a calm area of at least 30% for type
- Text contrast is checked against the actual pixels behind it
- Subject kept clear of the safe-area edge

**Never**
- Text across faces or busy detail
- A colour wash so heavy the subject is lost

## Framed photograph

The picture sits in a window on the grid.

**Do**
- The frame aligns to the grid on at least two edges
- Crop to the subject, not to the centre of the file

**Never**
- A frame larger than the picture deserves
- Abutting opaque shapes that leave seams

## Cut-out object or person

The subject removed from its background and placed on colour.

**Do**
- Clean edge with no halo
- A grounding shadow in the scene's light direction
- Scale chosen for drama

**Never**
- Cutting out hair or glass badly
- Floating objects with no shadow

## Duotone

A photograph mapped to two palette colours.

**Do**
- Use for texture and mood, on places and objects
- Keep enough tonal range to read the subject

**Never**
- On faces where skin tone carries identity
- To disguise an irrelevant stock photo

## Collage

Several cut pictures combined into one idea.

**Do**
- One anchor figure
- A shared paper palette
- Licences that allow modification and combination

**Never**
- Combining people so as to imply endorsement
- More than five pictures

## 3D render

A lit object or scene made in software.

**Do**
- Believable material and one key light
- Type in the same light
- Labelled when it is not a photograph of the real product

**Never**
- Showing a product the customer will not receive
- Property renders presented as photographs

## Illustration

Drawn imagery: line, flat shape or engraving.

**Do**
- One hand across a set
- Line weight matched to the type weight

**Never**
- Mixed illustration styles
- Clip-art standing in for an idea

## Pattern

A repeat that carries the surface.

**Do**
- Named source and permission where a pattern has cultural ownership
- A calm panel for the words
- Scale chosen so the repeat reads at the format's viewing distance

**Never**
- Pattern directly behind small text
- Sacred or restricted motifs as decoration
