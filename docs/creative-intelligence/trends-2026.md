## Tactile texture on screen

Grain, paper fibre and ink spread added to digital work so it feels printed and made.

- **Caution:** Texture over a weak layout is still a weak layout. Never on small text.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Oversized serif headlines

High-contrast serifs set very large, replacing the neutral sans as the default premium voice.

- **Caution:** Needs a true display serif; we ship two. Hairlines fail at small sizes and on billboards.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Named places and local detail

Work that names its street, cloth, dish or dialect rather than a generic regional look.

- **Caution:** Only with real ties and permission. Never borrow another community's symbols.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Unretouched, direct-light photography

Flash, daylight and visible skin texture in place of airbrushed studio polish.

- **Caution:** Still needs consent, good framing and correct exposure for darker skin tones.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Rendered product worlds

Products staged in impossible lit scenes, at monumental or miniature scale.

- **Caution:** Must be labelled when it is not a photograph of the product sold. Depends on decision D1.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Visible grids and system marks

Registration marks, column lines and data labels shown as part of the design.

- **Caution:** Labels must be true. No invented coordinates, codes or specifications.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Type built to move

Headlines designed first for short video, with the still as a frame from the loop.

- **Caution:** Create exports stills today. Motion belongs to a later phase.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Saturated colour blocking

Two or three flat, strong colours meeting on hard edges, without gradients.

- **Caution:** Check contrast in every pairing; equal-value colours vibrate.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Quiet restraint in premium work

Smaller marks, fewer words and material cues in place of logos and gold.

- **Caution:** Fails without an excellent photograph or object. Wrong for offers.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.

## Cut-out collage

Hand-cut photographs and paper shapes combined with clean type.

- **Caution:** Every source image needs a licence that allows cutting and combining.
- **Basis:** Editorial observation by the build team from published brand and campaign work; not a measured statistic.
