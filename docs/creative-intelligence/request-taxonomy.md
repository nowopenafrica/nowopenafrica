# Request taxonomy

> Generated from `src/lib/create/intel/requests.ts` by `npm run intel:docs`. Edit the registry, not this file; a test fails when they differ.

The seven kinds of thing people ask Create for. They sit above the catalogue categories and map onto them.


## Branding and identity

- **Catalogue categories:** brand, presentations

**Examples**
- Logo and visual identity
- Brand guidelines
- Business cards and stationery
- Company profile
- Brand launch campaign

**The brief must settle**
- What the business is and for whom
- Three words it should feel like
- Existing assets to keep
- Where the identity will be seen most

## Social media and digital marketing

- **Catalogue categories:** social

**Examples**
- Instagram posts and carousels
- Stories and reels covers
- Facebook and LinkedIn creatives
- Thumbnails
- Product launch posts

**The brief must settle**
- Platform and format
- The one thing the post says
- The action wanted
- Whether it is one post or a series

## Advertising and outdoor

- **Catalogue categories:** advertising

**Examples**
- Billboards
- Transit and bus wraps
- Digital screens
- Display ads
- Shop signs

**The brief must settle**
- Exact placement and size
- Viewing distance and time
- The single message, in seven words or fewer
- Brand sign-off

## Events and entertainment

- **Catalogue categories:** events

**Examples**
- Event posters and invitations
- Conference identities
- Stage and screen graphics
- Speaker cards
- Programmes and tickets

**The brief must settle**
- Name, date, time and venue
- Who is appearing, with confirmed names
- How to attend
- The full set of pieces needed

## Packaging and product design

- **Catalogue categories:** product

**Examples**
- Food and drink packaging
- Cosmetics and skincare
- Labels
- Bottles and boxes
- Shopping bags

**The brief must settle**
- Exact dieline or size
- Mandatory label information for the market
- Material and print method
- Where it is sold and beside what

## Print and merchandise

- **Catalogue categories:** print, product

**Examples**
- Flyers, posters and brochures
- Menus and price lists
- T-shirts, caps and uniforms
- Mugs, bottles and bags
- Stickers

**The brief must settle**
- Finished size and quantity
- Print method and colours available
- The information it must carry
- How it will be handed out or worn

## Photography, video and motion

- **Catalogue categories:** production

**Examples**
- Product photography
- Portraits
- Short video and reels
- Motion graphics

**The brief must settle**
- What is being shot and where
- Where the result will be used
- Usage rights and consent
- Whether NowOpen can make this today or must refer it
