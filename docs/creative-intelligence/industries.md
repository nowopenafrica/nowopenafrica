<a id="restaurant-dining"></a>
## Restaurants and dining

*Food & Hospitality*

- **Audience:** Diners choosing where to eat tonight or this weekend; groups and couples.
- **Tone:** Appetising, confident, specific about the dish.
- **Movements that fit:** Editorial, Organic Natural, Monochrome Photographic, Information Design
- **Usually wrong:** Gradient Futurism, 3D and CGI, Neo-Brutalism
- **Often needs:** Opening announcement post, Menu, Table card, Poster, Story set

**Goals**
- Make the food look worth the trip
- Say where and when
- Get a booking or a visit

**Must carry**
- Name and area
- What kind of food
- Opening date or hours
- How to book or order

**Imagery**
- The actual dishes, shot close in daylight
- The room with people in it
- Hands, fire, ingredients

**Cautions**
- Never use a photo of food the kitchen does not serve
- Alcohol promotion has rules
- Allergen and price accuracy
